\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Across a triple-robustness (embedder $\times$ corpus $\times$ judge)
analysis of five RAG architectures: \emph{over-citation is
setting-universal}; \emph{its faithfulness consequence is
corpus-conditional}; \emph{stratum winners are embedder-robust but
corpus-conditional}; and \emph{single-judge verdicts are
retrieval-state- and date-fragile}. Triple-robustness is the
minimum bar for RAG architecture claims.
