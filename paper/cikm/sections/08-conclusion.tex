\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
We presented a triple-robustness analysis (embedder $\times$ corpus
$\times$ judge) of five RAG architectures for multi-hop traceability.
\emph{Over-citation is setting-universal}; \emph{its faithfulness
consequence is corpus-conditional}; \emph{stratum winners are
embedder-robust but corpus-conditional}; \emph{single-judge verdicts
are retrieval-state- and date-fragile}. Triple-robustness is the
minimum defensible bar for RAG architecture claims.
