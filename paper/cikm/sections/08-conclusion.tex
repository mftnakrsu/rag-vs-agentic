\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Varying embedder, corpus, traversal and judge around a fixed
five-pipeline matrix: \emph{graph expansion floods the context but the
synthesizer cites selectively, and moving the measurement point from
context to answer inverts the architecture ranking} -- in all six
settings, under a changed traversal, and against two mitigations;
\emph{the faithfulness decline follows context expansion rather than the
corpus}, a correction to the corpus-conditional reading we previously
reported; \emph{stratum winners are embedder- and seed-robust}; and
\emph{judges drift on frozen inputs and disagree systematically across
vendors while $\kappa$ hides it}. Robustness on these axes -- and an
explicit choice of citation-measurement point -- is the minimum bar for
RAG architecture claims.
